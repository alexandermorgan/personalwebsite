I'm a music theorist turned software developer. I like building things for the web, teaching computers to read music, and making tools that do more with less.

I have a PhD in music theory from McGill University, where my dissertation used computers to study how Renaissance treatises taught counterpoint. That led to computational musicology: with Craig Sapp I created the Renaissance dissonance classifier in his Humlib and worked on automatic cadence detection with the Citations: The Renaissance Imitation Mass project. More recently I've worked on tokenization for language models with BatchBPE, and on hypermedia-driven web apps like Hypermedia Jobs.
