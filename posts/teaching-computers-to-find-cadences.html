A cadence is a moment of musical closure, and in Renaissance polyphony most cadences are built around a dissonant suspension resolving to a perfect interval. That makes them a good test of how well a computer can be taught a musical style.

Interval successions

The approach described in my CRIM essay uses only the succession of intervals between pairs of voices, which turns out to be enough to find cadential voice functions with high accuracy.
