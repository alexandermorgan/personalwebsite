Every page on this site is plain HTML rendered by a single Cloudflare Worker. When you follow a link from one page to another, a cut-down copy of fixi.js fetches the new page and swaps in just its main content, so the header and footer stay put.

Why fixi

fixi is a tiny library for hypermedia controls: a few fx- attributes tell it what to fetch and where to put the result. This site uses only a handful of its features, so the vendored copy has everything else removed.
